About Us

Founded in 2024 by Dorcus Maremele, KG Buffalo is a proudly South African automotive service provider specializing in high-quality car care solutions. The company was created to meet the growing need for reliable spare key services and vehicle protection technologies. As a locally owned business, we are committed to offering convenient, efficient, and affordable solutions to drivers across Limpopo and beyond.

Our core services include car spare key cutting and programming—covering traditional keys, transponders, and smart keys. Whether you've lost your key or simply need a spare, KG Buffalo ensures quick turnaround times and professional precision with every service. In addition to our key services, we also offer car rubberizing solutions, which protect both the underbody of your vehicle and the back of your bakkie (load bin) from rust, corrosion, and harsh road conditions.

At KG Buffalo, we pride ourselves on being more than just a service provider—we’re your trusted vehicle protection partner.
