About Us

Founded in 2024 by Dorcus Maremele, KG Buffalo is a proudly South African automotive service provider specializing in high-quality car care solutions. The company offers spare key services and rubberizing for vehicle protection.

Our core services include key cutting & programming (traditional and smart keys) and rubberizing for underbody and load bin protection against rust and wear. We’re your trusted vehicle protection partner.
